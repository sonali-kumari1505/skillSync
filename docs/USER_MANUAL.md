# SkillSync User Manual & Student Guide

Welcome to **SkillSync**, the AI-powered student skill exchange platform!

---

## 1. Getting Started & Sign Up
1. Navigate to the SkillSync home page.
2. Click **Get Started** or **Sign Up**.
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - University/College Email
   - Password (minimum 6 characters)
   - College Name (e.g. DTU, IIT, BITS)
   - Department/Major (e.g. Computer Science, Design)
   - Academic Year (1st Year through Masters / PhD)
4. Submit the registration form. You will be automatically signed in and taken to your student dashboard.

---

## 2. Managing Your Skills & Profile
1. Click your profile avatar in the navigation bar and select **My Student Profile** (or go to `/profile`).
2. **Add Skills You Can Teach**:
   - Click **Add** under "Skills You Teach".
   - Enter skill name (e.g. Python, UI/UX Design, C++, French).
   - Set your proficiency level from 1 (Beginner) to 5 (Expert).
3. **Add Skills You Want to Learn**:
   - Click **Add** under "Skills You Want".
   - Specify target skill and target proficiency.
4. **Set Study Availability & Mode**:
   - Specify your availability (e.g. Weekends, Weekday Evenings) and preferred learning mode (Online, In-Person, or Hybrid).
5. Click **Save Profile Changes**.

---

## 3. Finding AI-Recommended Learning Partners
1. Navigate to **AI Matches** (`/recommendations`) in the navbar.
2. The scikit-learn recommendation engine calculates a bilateral compatibility percentage (0–100%) by evaluating:
   - What you teach ⇄ What they want to learn
   - What they teach ⇄ What you want to learn
   - Proficiency equilibrium & study availability
3. Review the **AI Recommendation Evidence & Insights** for each suggested peer.
4. Click **Initiate Exchange Request** to propose a collaborative session!

---

## 4. Initiating & Managing Exchange Requests
1. When viewing a student card or AI match, click **Exchange**.
2. Select the skill you will teach, the skill you want to learn, and propose a session schedule.
3. Submit the request. The recipient will receive an in-app notification.
4. View incoming and outgoing requests under **Requests** (`/requests`).
5. **Accepting a Request**:
   - When you accept a request, real-time messaging unlocks instantly, and an interactive learning roadmap is created!

---

## 5. Real-Time Chat & Session Coordination
1. Click **Chat** (`/chat`) in the navbar.
2. Select an active conversation from your accepted exchange partners list.
3. Chat in real-time, share study links, and coordinate session timings.
4. Typing indicators and online status badges indicate when your partner is active.

---

## 6. Interactive Learning Roadmaps & Milestones
1. Go to **Roadmaps** (`/progress`).
2. Track key learning objectives for your exchange.
3. Check off completed milestones to update your progress bar.
4. Add custom milestones and post collaborative study notes.

---

## 7. Ratings and Feedback
1. Once an exchange session is completed, navigate to **Requests** and click **Mark Completed**.
2. Click **Write Review**.
3. Select a rating from 1 to 5 stars and enter written feedback.
4. Your review is permanently attached to the student's verified profile rating.

---

## 8. Administrator Moderation (Admins Only)
1. Sign in with an administrator account (e.g., `admin@skillsync.edu`).
2. Navigate to `/admin`.
3. Monitor platform activity, suspend or reactivate student accounts, and resolve reported incidents.
